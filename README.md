Step 16 — Student Reflection
1. What is the purpose of a brand guide?
A brand guide provides clear rules for colours, fonts, images, tone and overall design style. It helps different materials look connected and professional.
2. How did Magic Media support the campaign?
Magic Media helped create an aviation-related visual quickly based on the campaign requirements. It provided a realistic starting image that could be edited and adapted for different formats.
3. What problems appeared after expanding the image?
The expanded image required checking for distorted passengers, repeated objects and changes in the airport background. Some generated details could look unnatural after the canvas was extended.
4. What element did you change using Magic Edit?
I edited an unclear airport display and changed it into a cleaner, non-readable display so that it would not appear to provide false flight information.
5. Why must AI-generated image text be checked?
AI-generated image text can contain spelling mistakes, meaningless characters or incorrect information. Checking it prevents misleading or unprofessional content from being published.
6. Why should baggage and security rules come from official sources?
Baggage and security requirements can vary by airline, airport, destination and journey. Official sources provide the appropriate and current information, so AI should not be relied upon to invent or confirm such rules.
7. How did you maintain consistency across three formats?
I used the same campaign name, colours, fonts, visual style and core message across the social-media post, poster and presentation visual. I changed only the layout and amount of information according to the platform.
8. What changes were required after resizing?
After resizing, I checked text size, image cropping, spacing and alignment. Some elements needed to be repositioned so that the final designs remained readable and balanced.
9. How did peer review improve your campaign?
Peer review helped identify areas that could be improved, particularly spacing, readability and the visibility of important information. I used the feedback to make the final campaign clearer and more professional.
10. Why must AI-assisted designs be reviewed before publication?
AI can create inaccurate text, unrealistic objects, misleading information or unintended visual details. Human review is therefore necessary to ensure that the final design is accurate, professional, appropriate and responsible.
